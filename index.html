It started with a PDF.

A small task. A Google search. One unexpected beginning.

I needed to convert some files to PDF. So, like anyone would, I went looking for a PDF editor on Google.

02 Built with intention

The tool worked. The details mattered.

Toolbox began as something I wanted for myself: a direct, dependable way to get a job done without working around somebody else's idea of how it should feel.

I have no interest in writing code for its own sake. I care about building things with intention — thinking through the structure, the interaction, and the details until the result feels inevitable.

03 One idea led to another

The base was there. The ideas kept coming.

Once the foundation was in place, the larger opportunity became clear. Useful tools across the web are often scattered between different interfaces, accounts, and standards.

A file converter became a coherent environment for the things worth doing every day — connected, consistent, and designed to work as one.

The idea becameToolbox.

Why Toolbox

Pro-grade tools. One quiet, fast place.

Every tool is built to the standard of the best dedicated app in its category, then made to work with all the others.

Assistant

An agent that uses every tool.

Ask in plain words. It reads your PDFs, Word files and email, runs calculators, builds websites with a live preview, drafts replies and schedules work, then shows its results as cards you can act on.

Private by design

Your files stay on your device.

PDF, image, audio and video work runs in the browser. Nothing is uploaded to process it.

Principles

Four promises, kept everywhere.

Fast

Tools open instantly and work in the browser. No installs, no queues, no waiting on a server to convert your file.

Private

Your documents, photos and recordings are processed on your device. What you open here stays yours.

Crafted

One design language across every tool: the same controls, the same shortcuts, the same care in the details.

Connected

Tools hand work to each other. Scan, clean, sign and send a document without downloading it once.
