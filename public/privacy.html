Privacy Policy

Last updated 23 September 2026

Toolbox (“we”, “the app”) is a collection of web tools at toolbox.meyigbenee.workers.dev. Most tools run entirely in your browser and never send your files or text anywhere. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account details. If you create an account: your email address, display name, username and chosen profile picture. Accounts are managed by our authentication provider, Supabase.
  • Content you choose to save. Files, notes and settings you save stay in your browser’s local storage unless a feature explicitly syncs them to your account.
  • Assistant requests. Messages you send to the Assistant are forwarded to the AI provider that answers them (for example Google Gemini) and are not used by us for any other purpose.
  • Mail data, only if you connect Gmail or Outlook — see below.
  • Basic technical logs kept by our hosting providers (Cloudflare and Render), such as IP address and request time, for security and reliability.

Gmail and Google user data

The Mail tool lets you read, organise and send your email inside Toolbox. When you connect a Google account we request:

  • gmail.modify — to list and open your messages and to mark, label, archive or delete them when you ask;
  • gmail.send — to send email you write in Toolbox;
  • userinfo.email — to show which account is connected.

We use this access only to display your mailbox to you and to carry out the actions you take in the Mail tool. Specifically:

  • We do not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone, except as needed to provide the Mail feature, to comply with law, or with your explicit consent.
  • We do not use Google user data to develop, improve or train generalised AI or machine-learning models, and we do not send your email to the Assistant or any AI provider.
  • Humans do not read your email, except with your explicit permission for a specific message, where required for security or legal reasons, or when the data is aggregated and anonymised for internal operations.
  • Message content is fetched from Google when you view it and is not stored on our servers. We store only the access and refresh tokens needed to keep your account connected, linked to your Toolbox account.

Toolbox’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Microsoft (Outlook) accounts are handled the same way, using Microsoft Graph permissions Mail.ReadWrite, Mail.Send and User.Read.

Disconnecting and deleting data

  • You can revoke Toolbox’s access at any time from your Google Account permissions page or your Microsoft account settings.
  • To have your stored mail tokens or your whole Toolbox account deleted, email us from the address on the account and we will do so within 30 days.

Service providers

We rely on Cloudflare (hosting), Render (API server), Supabase (accounts and database), Google and Microsoft (mail, sign-in) and the AI provider behind the Assistant. Each processes data only to provide its service to us.

Security

All traffic is encrypted with HTTPS. OAuth secrets and tokens are kept server-side and never exposed to other users.

Children

Toolbox is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above, and for material changes we will tell you in the app.
